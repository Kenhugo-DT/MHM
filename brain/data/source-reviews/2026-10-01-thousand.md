# 1000-node checkpoint: reviewed addition

The local graph had 923 nodes before this batch. This addition targets dated 1940s-1980s recordings and documented band lineups, not a numeric target alone. Every new node has a source and at least one sourced edge. The year on a member node anchors a cited recording; it is not claimed as the year that person joined the group.

## Sources and method

- [Library of Congress National Recording Registry](https://www.loc.gov/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/recording-registry/complete-national-recording-registry-listing/) supplies recording titles, principal acts, and years for the recording-context nodes. Its categories are not always identical to this map's broader genre zones; the edges deliberately say "nearby tradition" rather than quoting a Registry genre.
- [Registry descriptions and essays](https://www.loc.gov/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/recording-registry/descriptions-and-essays/) and [participant credits](https://www.loc.gov/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/recording-registry/complete-national-recording-registry-listing/additional-information/) provide the collaborative details.
- The linked Library of Congress essays in `curated-thousand.mjs` name members for Jefferson Airplane, The Cars, Talking Heads, ABBA, Eurythmics, Chic, Fleetwood Mac, R.E.M., Earth, Wind & Fire, and The Velvet Underground. [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame](https://rockhall.com/inductees/beach-boys/) identifies the Beach Boys members.

## Boundaries

- A group's recording does not make every member a solo recording artist. Member nodes use `member_of`, not a fabricated solo-release edge.
- The Velvet Underground is linked near alternative rock because the Registry describes its later influence there; this is a retrospective bridge, not a claim that the 1967 record was marketed as alternative rock.
- This is local curated preview data only. No Git push or Supabase/live import belongs to this checkpoint. Data and layouts must be deployed together when publication is explicitly requested.
