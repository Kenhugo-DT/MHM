# Marko Hietala

## Summary

Marko Tapani "Marco" Hietala (born 14 January 1966) is a Finnish heavy metal musician. Internationally, he is best known as the former bassist, male vocalist and secondary composer to Tuomas Holopainen, of the symphonic metal band Nightwish.
