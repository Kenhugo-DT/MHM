# Blind Willie McTell

## Summary

Twelve-string guitarist whose Statesboro Blues recording dates to 1928.

## Connections

- [[blues|Blues]] - Statesboro Blues (McTell's 1928 record documents his twelve-string blues style.)
- [[allman-brothers-band|The Allman Brothers Band]] - later Statesboro Blues version (The Allman Brothers later recorded a version of McTell's song, with Taj Mahal's 1968 arrangement as a key intermediary.)
