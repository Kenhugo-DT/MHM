# Big Joe Turner

## Summary

Blues and jump singer whose work bridged jazz, swing, R&B and early rock and roll.

## Connections

- [[rhythm-and-blues|Rhythm and blues]] - jump blues to R&B (Turner's blend of blues, jazz and swing fed his R&B recordings.)
