# Aretha Franklin

## Summary

Soul singer whose gospel background met rhythm and blues, jazz and pop.

## Connections

- [[gospel-music|Gospel music]] - gospel roots (Franklin sang in church before her secular career; gospel remained a vocal foundation.)
- [[pop-music|Pop music]] - pop crossover (Franklin's soul recordings also reached a broad pop audience.)
- [[rhythm-and-blues|Rhythm and blues]] - R&B voice (Franklin's recordings brought gospel-inflected singing into R&B.)
- [[soul-music|Soul music]] - soul singer (Franklin became a defining voice of soul, not merely a generic pop performer.)

## Curator Notes

Franklin's gospel upbringing and R&B recordings explain her soul and pop connections; a gospel influence is not the same as a later secular genre label.
