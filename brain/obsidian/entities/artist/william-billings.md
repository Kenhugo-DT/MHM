# William Billings

## Summary

American composer whose New England Psalm Singer appeared in 1770; his choral work remained in later shape-note singing.

## Connections

- [[folk|Folk]] - community hymnody (His published choral music continued in American community singing traditions.)
- [[shape-note-singing|Shape-note singing]] - hymns preserved (Billings's music remained in the later Southern shape-note repertory; he did not originate that practice.)
