# W. C. Handy

## Summary

Bandleader who combined blues and ragtime in a 1909 Memphis campaign song later published as The Memphis Blues.

## Connections

- [[blues|Blues]] - published blues (Handy collected blues and combined its sound with ragtime in The Memphis Blues.)
- [[ragtime|Ragtime]] - blues-ragtime blend (The 1909 campaign song blended blues features with popular ragtime.)
- [[william-grant-still|William Grant Still]] - worked as arranger (Still's first published arrangement was for W. C. Handy.)
