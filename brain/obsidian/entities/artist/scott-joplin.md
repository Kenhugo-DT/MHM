# Scott Joplin

## Summary

Ragtime composer whose Maple Leaf Rag appeared in 1899.

## Connections

- [[james-scott-ragtime|James Scott]] - publisher introduction (Joplin introduced Scott to publisher John Stark.)
- [[joseph-lamb|Joseph Lamb]] - arranged by Joplin (Joplin persuaded John Stark to publish Lamb's work and was credited as an arranger.)
- [[ragtime|Ragtime]] - Maple Leaf Rag (Joplin's 1899 composition became a major ragtime publication.)
