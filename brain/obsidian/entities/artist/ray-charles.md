# Ray Charles

## Summary

Singer and pianist who combined gospel and R&B into soul while also recording country and jazz material.

## Connections

- [[country|Country]] - country recordings (Charles recorded country songs as part of a deliberately cross-genre career.)
- [[gospel-music|Gospel music]] - gospel vocabulary (Gospel was a source for Charles's secular music; this edge describes influence, not a gospel-only career.)
- [[rhythm-and-blues|Rhythm and blues]] - R&B recordings (Charles brought gospel phrasing into rhythm and blues recordings.)
- [[soul-music|Soul music]] - soul pioneer (Charles's fusion of gospel expression and R&B helped define soul.)
