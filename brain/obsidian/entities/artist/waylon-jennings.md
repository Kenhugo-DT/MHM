# Waylon Jennings

## Summary

Country singer whose campaign for control over recordings helped define the Outlaw movement.

## Connections

- [[country|Country]] - country singer (Jennings remained a country artist while challenging Nashville recording practices.)
- [[outlaw-country|Outlaw country]] - Outlaw movement (Jennings fought for control of songs, sessions and musicians, a core Outlaw-country distinction.)
- [[willie-nelson|Willie Nelson]] - Waylon and Willie (Jennings and Nelson recorded together and became a paired public face of Outlaw country.)
