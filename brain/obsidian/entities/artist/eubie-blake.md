# Eubie Blake

## Summary

Pianist and composer of the 1921 musical Shuffle Along with Noble Sissle.

## Connections

- [[noble-sissle|Noble Sissle]] - Shuffle Along (Sissle wrote lyrics and Blake music for their 1921 musical.)
- [[ragtime|Ragtime]] - ragtime to stage (Blake's early piano language and 1921 show carried syncopated music into Broadway theater.)
