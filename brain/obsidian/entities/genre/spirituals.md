# Spirituals

## Summary

African American sacred songs passed down orally during slavery; 1855 marks an early written account, not the tradition's origin.

## Connections

- [[fisk-jubilee-singers|Fisk Jubilee Singers]] - sang spirituals (The ensemble brought spirituals to wider audiences on its 1871 tours.)
- [[gospel-music|Gospel music]] - sacred-music lineage (Spirituals predate the later African American gospel tradition; they are distinct forms.)
- [[hall-johnson|Hall Johnson]] - choral arrangements (Johnson formed a choir in 1925 dedicated to presenting spirituals.)
- [[tuskegee-institute-singers|Tuskegee Institute Singers]] - recorded spiritual (The ensemble recorded I've Been Buked and I've Been Scorned in 1917 or 1918.)
