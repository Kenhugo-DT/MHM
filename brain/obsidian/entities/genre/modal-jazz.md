# Modal jazz

## Summary

Jazz improvisation organized around modes rather than rapid chord changes, associated with Miles Davis and John Coltrane.

## Connections

- [[jazz|Jazz]] - jazz direction (Modal jazz changes the improvisational framework inside jazz.)
- [[john-coltrane|John Coltrane]] - modal improvisation (Coltrane later used modal improvisation in major recordings; his connection is not limited to Davis's band.)
- [[miles-davis|Miles Davis]] - modal turn (Davis moved toward modal improvisation on Kind of Blue after his earlier cool-jazz work.)

## Sources

- [National Endowment for the Arts](https://www.arts.gov/honors/jazz/miles-davis)

## Curator Notes

Modal jazz describes an improvisational approach, not a claim that all Davis or Coltrane recordings use modes.
