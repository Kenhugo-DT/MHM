# Cool jazz

## Summary

A post-bebop jazz direction associated with the arranged sound of Miles Davis's Birth of the Cool sessions.

## Connections

- [[jazz|Jazz]] - jazz direction (Cool jazz is a direction within jazz, not a separate origin tradition.)
- [[miles-davis|Miles Davis]] - Birth of the Cool (Davis's late-1940s nonet recordings are an anchor for cool jazz.)

## Sources

- [National Endowment for the Arts](https://www.arts.gov/honors/jazz/miles-davis)

## Curator Notes

Birth of the Cool is a specific Davis-led jazz phase. Keep it within jazz and distinct from his later modal and electric phases.
