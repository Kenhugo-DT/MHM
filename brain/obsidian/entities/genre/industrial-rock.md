# industrial rock

## Summary

Industrial rock is a fusion genre that fuses industrial music and rock music. Originally emerging in the late 1970s to early 1980s pioneered by artists such as American bands Chrome, Swans and Big Black, England's Killing Joke, and Canadian outfit Skinny Puppy, the movement was further proliferated in the late 1980s by Wax Trax!
