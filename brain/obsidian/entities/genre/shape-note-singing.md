# Shape-note singing

## Summary

American community hymn-singing tradition documented in the 1835 Southern Harmony songbook; the practice is older.

## Connections

- [[folk|Folk]] - communal folk hymnody (Shape-note books supported a continuing American communal singing tradition.)
- [[william-billings|William Billings]] - hymns preserved (Billings's music remained in the later Southern shape-note repertory; he did not originate that practice.)
