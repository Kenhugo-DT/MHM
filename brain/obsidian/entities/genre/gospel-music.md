# Gospel music

## Summary

African American sacred music whose vocal and rhythmic practices shaped R&B, soul and early rock and roll.

## Curator Notes

Keep gospel a distinct sacred tradition. Its documented influence on R&B and soul is a historical bridge, not evidence that the genres are interchangeable.
