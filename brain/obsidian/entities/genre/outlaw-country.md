# Outlaw country

## Summary

1970s country movement centered on performers taking greater control of their recordings and repertoire.

## Connections

- [[country|Country]] - country movement (Outlaw country was a 1970s movement within country, distinguished partly by artist control.)
- [[waylon-jennings|Waylon Jennings]] - Outlaw movement (Jennings fought for control of songs, sessions and musicians, a core Outlaw-country distinction.)
- [[willie-nelson|Willie Nelson]] - Outlaw movement (Nelson helped establish the 1970s Outlaw country movement.)

## Sources

- [Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum](https://countrymusichalloffame.org/hall-of-fame/waylon-jennings)

## Curator Notes

Outlaw country is partly about control over material, studios and musicians. Do not infer that all outlaw artists share one fixed musical style.
