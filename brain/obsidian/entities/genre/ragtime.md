# Ragtime

## Summary

Syncopated African American music that emerged in published form in the mid-1890s and helped shape early jazz.

## Connections

- [[american-popular-song|American popular song]] - sheet-music crossover (Published ragtime circulated through the commercial popular-song market.)
- [[claude-debussy|Claude Debussy]] - cakewalk crossover (His 1908 Golliwogg's Cake-Walk reflects ragtime's reach into European concert music.)
- [[eubie-blake|Eubie Blake]] - ragtime to stage (Blake's early piano language and 1921 show carried syncopated music into Broadway theater.)
- [[irving-berlin|Irving Berlin]] - ragtime-era song (The Library of Congress catalogs Alexander's Ragtime Band among ragtime music.)
- [[james-p-johnson|James P. Johnson]] - ragtime-to-stride (Stride piano drew on earlier ragtime techniques without being identical to ragtime.)
- [[james-reese-europe|James Reese Europe]] - syncopated popular music (Europe worked across Black popular music and social dance before jazz was widely named.)
