# Western swing

## Summary

Southwestern dance music joining country fiddles with jazz, blues and big-band swing.

## Connections

- [[bakersfield-sound|Bakersfield sound]] - western swing roots (Western swing was one of the ingredients in Owens's Bakersfield sound.)
- [[blues|Blues]] - blues roots (Blues was one ingredient of western swing rather than its whole sound.)
- [[bob-wills|Bob Wills]] - western swing bandleader (Wills and his Texas Playboys are a documented anchor of western swing.)
- [[buck-owens|Buck Owens]] - western swing background (Owens learned western swing as a working Southwestern dance-band musician.)
- [[country|Country]] - country dance music (Western swing developed in the Southwestern country-dance tradition.)
- [[jazz|Jazz]] - jazz phrasing (Western swing incorporated New Orleans jazz and improvising dance-band practices.)
- [[swing-music|Swing music]] - big-band swing bridge (Big-band swing was another ingredient in the western-swing mixture.)
