# Map Zones

These are the current high-level map zones. They are layout hints, not hard
truths. Bridge nodes can belong to one primary zone and several secondary zones.

## roots-blues

Roots, blues, early electric guitar history and rock and roll foundations.

## folk-country-vise

Folk, country, acoustic traditions, jazz-adjacent guitar and Norwegian vise.

## rock-circuit

The central rock web: classic rock, glam, major guitar bands and bridge acts.

## psychedelia-prog

Psychedelia, art rock, progressive rock, space rock and progressive metal.

## hard-rock-metal

Hard rock, heavy metal, doom, thrash, power metal and metal-adjacent scenes.

## punk-alt

Punk, post-punk, hardcore, alternative rock and adjacent underground scenes.

## guitar-workshop

Guitar models, makers, brands and instrument-history connections.
